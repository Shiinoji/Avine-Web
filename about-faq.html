GOOD TO KNOW

A few common questions .

Can't find what you're looking for? Just ask us ↗

How long does delivery take? +

Metro Manila orders usually arrive in 1–3 business days; provincial deliveries typically take 3–7 business days. Delivery times can vary for remote areas and during peak periods. Delivery is free on orders over ₱5,000, and we'll share tracking details when your order is on its way.

What does the warranty cover? +

Every Avine purchase includes a 2-year limited warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. If something doesn't feel right, reach out and our team will help make it right.

Can I return or exchange an item? +

Absolutely. You have 30 days from delivery to request a return or exchange for an unused item in its original packaging. Contact our local support team and we'll guide you through the process. Return conditions and any applicable fees will be confirmed before you send an item back.

How do I get in touch with support? +

Our support team is here Monday–Friday, 9:00am–6:00pm Philippine Time (PHT). Email us at hello@avine.ph or use the contact form . We aim to respond within one business day.

Where do you deliver? +

We deliver to serviceable addresses across the Philippines. Availability and delivery times may vary by location. Check your address at checkout or contact our team if you'd like help confirming coverage.
